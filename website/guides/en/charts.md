# Choose a chart and map its fields

Begin with the result table. Identify the dimension that labels each group and the numeric measure to plot. For sales by channel, channel is the dimension and summed sales is the measure.

## Compare categories or time periods

Use a bar chart to compare channels and a line chart for a dated series. Group dates at a consistent interval before plotting. Use a number card for a single total and a table when the reader needs exact rows. For a pie chart, use mutually exclusive categories whose values form a meaningful whole.

## Verify the mapping

Check the axis labels, units and tooltip against a known row. If one series looks flat, inspect the numeric field and date order. Save the visualization with its analysis so a dashboard card uses the same result.
