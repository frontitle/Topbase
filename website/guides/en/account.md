# Set your profile, language and sign-in methods

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My account**. Changes here apply to your own account. For example, you can work in English while a colleague reads the same dashboard in Chinese.

## Change your name, avatar or language

1. Under **Profile &amp; photo**, enter the name you want colleagues to see. To change your photo, choose **Upload picture** and select a PNG, JPEG or WebP image. Check the cropped preview, or choose **Remove** to use your initials.
2. Choose **English** or **简体中文** in **Language**. Select **Save profile** and reopen the account menu to check the new name and language. Labels you supplied for analyses, tables and dashboards keep their original spelling.
3. To change your sign-in email, enter the new email address and your current password in the field that appears. Save the change and use the updated email on your next sign-in.

## Change a password

Open **Sign-in & security**. Fill in the current password, a new password of at least eight characters and the same new password again. Select **Update password** and look for the saved confirmation. If the two new entries differ, enter them again. The status at the top indicates whether the workspace permits password sign-in.

## Connect a workplace identity

1. Open **Linked accounts** and find the provider enabled by your administrator. Choose **Link account**, follow the provider's authorization screen, then return to the same Topbase account.
2. Reopen the tab and check the **Bound** status. An organization-managed identity is maintained by the administrator. If a provider is unavailable, the administrator can check its configuration under **People & groups**.
3. To remove a binding, select its **Unbind** action and confirm the provider name. When password sign-in is disabled and this is your only method, bind a second available method first.

For a personal automation credential, open the separate **AI and API** tab and follow [Connect an AI client](ai-integrations.html).
